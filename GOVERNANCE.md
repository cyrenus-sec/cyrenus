# Cyrenus Project Governance

## Overview

Cyrenus is an open-source, vendor-neutral network security project.  
The project follows an open governance model designed to encourage community participation and long-term sustainability.

---

## Project Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Making technical decisions
- Managing releases
- Ensuring adherence to security and quality standards

Initial maintainers are listed in the repository and may evolve over time.

---

### Contributors

Anyone may contribute to Cyrenus by:
- Submitting issues
- Proposing documentation improvements
- Opening pull requests

Contributors do not need prior approval to contribute.

---

## Decision-Making Process

Cyrenus uses a **lazy consensus** model:

- Decisions are made through public discussion (GitHub Issues, PRs, Discussions).
- If no objections are raised within a reasonable time, the change is accepted.
- Significant changes require explicit approval from at least one maintainer.

In case of disagreement:
- Maintainers will seek consensus.
- If consensus cannot be reached, maintainers may vote.
- A simple majority decides.

---

## Becoming a Maintainer

A contributor may be invited to become a maintainer if they:
- Demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions
- Show good judgment in design and security decisions
- Act in the best interest of the project and community

The existing maintainers approve new maintainers by consensus.

---

## Code of Conduct

The project follows a Code of Conduct to ensure a respectful and inclusive community.  
All participants are expected to adhere to it.

---

## Scope of Authority

- Maintainers guide technical direction.
- No single individual or organization controls the project.
- All governance decisions are made transparently.

---

## Amendments

This governance document may be updated through the standard decision-making process described above.
